Information processed

Neon Serpent can be played offline. If you choose online multiplayer, the game sends your chosen nickname, room and gameplay messages to the multiplayer server so it can run your match. The server and its hosting provider also receive your IP address and ordinary connection metadata needed to deliver the service, limit abuse, and troubleshoot outages. Active rooms are held in server memory and may be cleared when the server restarts.

The app stores your settings and chosen appearance on your device. It does not require an account. The app does not include advertising or analytics by default. If a crash-reporting endpoint is configured for a release, technical error details may be sent to that endpoint; review its operator and retention policy before enabling it.

How information is used and shared

Multiplayer information is used to connect players, operate game rooms, protect the server, and diagnose service issues. Network requests are processed by the multiplayer hosting provider. We do not sell personal information. We do not provide in-game player chat.

Retention and choices

Online match state is temporary and stored in memory. Hosting logs may retain network metadata according to the host's policies. You can play offline without sending game data to the multiplayer server. You can change your nickname and settings in the app.

Children and security

The game includes stylized, non-graphic snake collisions and online multiplayer. Parents or guardians should supervise children's online use. Online connections use HTTPS/WSS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
